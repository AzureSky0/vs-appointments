Settings & backup

Backup

Your bookings are saved only on this phone. Save a backup file regularly (to WhatsApp, Google Drive or email) so they're safe if the phone is lost or the browser is cleared.

Services

One per line. These show up as quick choices in the booking form.

WhatsApp reminder message

Sent when you tap WhatsApp on a booking. You can use {name}, {date}, {time}, {services} and {place}.

Start fresh

Deletes every booking on this phone, for example to clear test bookings. Your services and WhatsApp message are kept. Save a backup first if you might need them.

How to use

Add a booking

Tap New booking at the bottom. Fill in the name, phone and date. Time, services, amounts and notes are optional. Tap Save booking.

For a client who has booked before, start typing their name and pick it from the list. Their number fills in by itself.

On each booking

Call rings the client. WhatsApp opens a chat with a reminder message already typed, so just tap send. Map opens the address in Google Maps. Done marks it finished. Edit changes or deletes it.

Tabs

Upcoming shows bookings from today onwards. Anything from an earlier day that isn't marked done stays at the top so you don't forget it.

Calendar shows the month. The number under a date is how many bookings that day has. Tap a date to see them, or + Add on this day.

Past has finished and cancelled bookings.

Money

Enter the Total and Advance paid when booking. Each card shows the balance, and the top of the screen shows the total still to collect. When the client pays the rest, tap Edit and change Advance to the full amount.

Two clashing bookings?

If you pick a time within 2 hours of another booking that day, the form shows a yellow warning. You can still save it.

Keep your bookings safe ⚠️

Bookings are saved only on this phone. Always open the app from the VS icon on your home screen, not from Safari.

Once a week, tap ⚙️ → Save backup file and send it to yourself on WhatsApp or save it to Files. If you get a new phone, add the app again and tap ⚙️ → Restore from backup file.

Excel sheet

⚙️ → Download as Excel sheet gives you all bookings in a spreadsheet, for example to count the month's work.
